Support

FreeMyStorage finds the photos and videos that take the most space on your iPhone, so you can delete the ones you don’t need and make large videos smaller.

Write to luismec90+freemystorage@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, its iOS version and FreeMyStorage’s version, which About shows: on the app’s main screen, tap More, the button with three dots at the top right, then About FreeMyStorage.

Common questions

I deleted photos and videos, but my iPhone has no more free space.

Photos keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and they take space there until then. To free the space now, tap Open Photos to empty it, in the sheet FreeMyStorage shows after deleting, or open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted and delete the items there. iOS doesn’t let apps empty Recently Deleted for you.

What does “Only in iCloud” mean?

The item’s original is only in iCloud, so it takes little space on your iPhone. Deleting it frees iCloud storage and removes it from all your devices.

Some photos and videos are missing.

If you allowed access to only some photos, FreeMyStorage can see only those. To let it find your largest ones, go to Settings > Apps > FreeMyStorage > Photos and choose Full Access. FreeMyStorage lists your 25 largest photos and videos, and Show 25 more, under the list, adds more, up to 100. It leaves out hidden photos and shared albums.

How do I stop FreeMyStorage from seeing my photos?

Go to Settings > Apps > FreeMyStorage > Photos and choose None. iOS restarts the app when you change this. The next time you open FreeMyStorage, it deletes from your iPhone what it measured. Your photos and videos stay as they are. Deleting the app also removes what it measured.

How do I make a video smaller?

Open the video full screen and tap Make smaller, in the panel at the bottom. FreeMyStorage suggests a lower quality, and shows the new size and the space saved for each one; tap Make smaller to start. The new video keeps its format and sound, when and where it was taken, and its albums. iOS then asks to delete the original, which moves to Recently Deleted. If your iPhone is short of space, FreeMyStorage says how much to free first.

Slow-motion, Cinematic and spatial videos, videos in formats such as ProRes, and videos synced from a computer can’t be made smaller.

How do I change FreeMyStorage’s language?

FreeMyStorage shows in English or Spanish, following your iPhone’s languages. To choose a language for FreeMyStorage alone, open About and tap Language, which opens FreeMyStorage’s settings. About shows Language when your iPhone lists more than one language in Settings > General > Language & Region.

Does FreeMyStorage upload my photos?

No. FreeMyStorage works on your iPhone and sends nothing over the internet. The Privacy Policy explains the details.

Requirements

FreeMyStorage needs an iPhone with iOS 27 or later.
